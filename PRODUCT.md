# Product

## Register

product

> Default register is **product** (member dashboard, bookings, admin). Brand surface (`/`, `/activities`, marketing) overrides to brand per-page.

## Users

- **Members** — hill walkers and hikers, aged 18–65, booking treks, viewing past trips, and managing payment status. They are on desktop and mobile, often booking from a phone mid-planner.
- **Trip leaders** — create and manage the trips they lead.
- **Admins** — club staff running operations: pricing tiers, bookings, community moderation.

## Product Purpose

The Hill Trekkers Club runs guided hikes and expeditions. Members discover trips, reserve a seat, and pay through Paystack; the club confirms seats only on paid bookings and releases unpaid holds. Success is a member who can browse → book → pay → confirm without friction, and know exactly where their money and seat stand.

## Brand Personality

Premium expedition brand. Three words: **authoritative, warm, exacting**. Voice is a knowledgeable mountain guide — confident, precise, never hyped. Emotional goal: calm confidence ("you're in good hands") plus genuine anticipation for the trail.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI "outdoor club" templates: pastel mountains, flat-illustration hikers, emoji-as-icons.
- Cheap-looking green/nature stock clichés; corporate booking-sites.
- Anything that reads as mass-market tour operator rather than a considered, tight-knit club.

## Design Principles

1. **Exactness over decoration.** Every element earns its place; real information density beats padding.
2. **Confidence at rest.** Strong forest/trail palette, generous whitespace, calm motion — no shouting.
3. **Money & seats are sacred.** Payment status, price locks, and seat counts are always unambiguous and front-and-center.
4. **Progress is visible.** From "no bookings yet" to "paid & confirmed", the user always knows the next step.
5. **Expedition-grade detail.** Haptic focus states, real empty/loading/error states, keyboard-complete flows.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast on light surfaces; large text ≥ 3:1.
- Reduced motion honored (no layout-property animation, crossfade fallbacks).
- Keyboard-complete flows for booking and payment; visible focus rings.
- Clear error and empty states; never leave a user guessing what to do next.
